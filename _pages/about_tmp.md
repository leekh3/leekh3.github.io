My name is Kyunghun Lee, and I'm a staff scientist at [Emotion and Development Branch (EDB)](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-conducted-at-nimh/research-areas/clinics-and-labs/edb) at the [National Institutes of Health (NIH)](https://www.nih.gov). In the lab, I focus on computational analysis and software design, and I assist NIH researchers at every stage of the automated software workflow. My work helps identify treatment targets using neuroscience, and I'm also involved in software and algorithm development based on real-time data from wearable devices / iPhone.

I earned my Ph.D. in [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://ece.umd.edu/) in 2018 from [the University of Maryland, College Park (UMD)](https://umd.edu/), under the guidance of [Professor Shuvra S. Bhattacharyya](https://user.eng.umd.edu/~ssb/). 

[//]: # (My research areas broadly include machine learning and software implementation on devices. Specifically, my research is focused on two major, inter-related sub-problems: algorithm development, which involves selecting and optimizing supervised/unsupervised algorithms, and system development, which involves mapping these algorithms to real-time, energy-efficient implementations on targeted platforms. I also develop model-based digital signal processing software.)

[//]: # (Following my Ph.D., I worked as a postdoctoral fellow in Diagnostic Radiology and Nuclear Medicine at the University of Maryland School of Medicine. My focus was on leveraging machine learning and computational modeling to gain a deeper understanding of the relationship between brain and behavior, leading to new therapeutic concepts for brain disorders and brain-inspired A.I. Currently, I'm working as a Staff Scientist at the National Institute of Mental Health &#40;NIMH&#41;.)
